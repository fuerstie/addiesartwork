Features

Everything you need to grow as an artist.

🎓

60 Structured Lessons

Five courses from Fundamentals to Sketching, each broken into short achievable steps. Learn at your own pace — every lesson unlocks the next.

🎯

Real-Time Stroke Scoring

Every stroke is measured for path accuracy and line confidence. A 0–100 score tells you exactly how close you are and where to improve.

🖼️

Illustrated Subject Gallery

17 subjects across 7 categories — Anime, Animals, Food, Vehicles, Nature, Portraits, Architecture. Step-by-step reveal or full freehand tracing mode.

📸

Photo Tracing

Import any photo from your library and draw over it. Adjust opacity, toggle the reference on and off, and save your finished piece back to Photos.

🔥

Daily Streaks & XP

Build a daily drawing habit with streak tracking, XP scoring, and a 7-day practice calendar. Progress is saved per lesson and per subject.

✏️

Apple Pencil Ready

Designed for natural pressure-sensitive drawing on iPad with Apple Pencil. Finger drawing on iPhone works great too.
