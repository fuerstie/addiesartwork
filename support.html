Support

Quick fixes, fast answers.

Most issues take less than a minute to fix. Find yours below, or email us and we will help.

The app freezes or won't open
  1. Force-quit the app: swipe up from the bottom (or double-click Home), find Addie's ArtWork, and swipe it away.
  2. Open the app again.
  3. If it still freezes, restart your iPhone or iPad, then try again.
  4. As a last resort, delete and reinstall the app from the App Store. Drawings you saved to Photos stay in Photos.
The eraser, undo, or redo isn't working the way I expect

Tap the eraser tool, then choose Precise to rub out just part of a line, or remove a whole line at once. Undo and redo work on every canvas, including the lessons in Courses. If a button looks greyed out, there is nothing to undo or redo yet, make a stroke first and try again.

I can't print or save a certificate
  1. Finish a drawing or a course, or open the Progress tab and tap My Certificates.
  2. Choose the certificate you want.
  3. Tap Print to send it to a printer, or Share / Save PDF to save it to Files or Photos.
  4. Your artist name on the certificate comes from Settings, tap the gear icon on the Home tab to set it.
I can't find the Thanksgiving or Christmas drawings

The holiday drawings are limited edition and only appear during their season: Thanksgiving from November 1 to 30, and Christmas from November 27 to January 1. They come back every year. Outside those dates they are hidden, so check back when the season starts.

My streak, XP, or progress isn't updating
  1. Finish a lesson all the way through, progress counts when a lesson or drawing is completed.
  2. Check the Progress tab to see your streak, XP, and lessons done.
  3. Make sure your device date and time are set to automatic in Settings, a wrong date can break streaks.
  4. Force-quit and reopen the app to refresh the display.
My Apple Pencil isn't drawing
  1. Make sure the Pencil is charged and paired: check Bluetooth in Settings.
  2. Try drawing with your finger. If finger drawing works, the issue is the Pencil, not the app.
  3. If your palm keeps making marks, turn on Apple Pencil only mode in Settings (gear icon on the Home tab).
  4. Remove any thick case that might block the Pencil from pairing, then reattach it.
I'm not getting the daily practice reminder

The reminder only runs if you turn it on in Settings (gear icon on the Home tab). The app asks for notification permission only when you enable it. Check that notifications are allowed for Addie's ArtWork in your device Settings, and that Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode isn't silencing it.

Where do I find Settings and my certificates?

Settings lives behind the gear icon on the Home tab. There you can set your artist name, pen and eraser sizes, guide strength, Apple Pencil only mode, haptics, the daily reminder, and light or dark theme. Your certificates live on the Progress tab under My Certificates, or right after you finish a drawing.
